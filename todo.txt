
Explore mini map:
    A small mini map on the bottom right of the screen, that will show
    earth and the location of the airplane on it, helping to get the full context,
    only on the explore mode.

    Size of that mini map (zoom, hight, width) will be set in the constant file,
    as well if the map is Stellite or not (you choose the map you think should be there).
