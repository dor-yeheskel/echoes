Right now - the navigation game is great.

However, I got this feedback:
"""
The game idea sounds quite interesting (no pun intended), but the gameplay with those goals and fuel and restarts lets my expectations down instantly. As a player, I don't want to have this challenge, I just want to fly around and explore things all by myself, listening to all the objects I stumble across.
Maybe it's nice to have routes as additional predefined interactive audio experiences, but don't interrupt the players by forcing them to restart from the beginning. Additionally many players, including me, can be quite illiterate geographically, so we don't know where the stuff is located, and would rather expect from the game to show us instead. Also it's hard to tell all these cities apart, since there are no countries on the map.
Overall the game has potential, I'd just say try to keep it as easy as possible to play and don't give players any reasons to leave.
"""

Let's add a separate “Explore Mode” alongside the existing game modes.

In this mode, disable fuel, restarts, and mission constraints to allow free roaming.
Keep the same map and movement, but make music-triggering locations subtly discoverable (e.g., light visual hints or proximity-based cues).
When the player reaches such a location, clearly present its name (and optionally country) and play the associated music.
The goal is to create a relaxed, exploration-focused experience without pressure, while keeping the core interaction intact.


The infrastructure should be the same - no new physics or mechanics needed, just a different mode
with adjusted rules and feedback - almost like a new level but without the option of failure.

I imagine the following:
UI:
    Above the main table on the menu - a clickable "Explore The World". 
    The main table will have a title: "Levels".
    If user clicks "Explore The World" - it will start.
    
    Of course they can navigate with the keyboard or mouse the same to choose level or explore mode.

Gameplay
    The airplane will be placed in constant location: Marseille, France, angle of 95 degrees (towards Rome).

    The title on the top will be: "Explore Mode: Free Roam".
    No fuel bar at all.
    The HUD: will have the last city with music that they encountered.
    The city hud that changed when you encounter a new city (any city) - will be the same as in the main game.

    Cities that will be shown:
        Every city in the world (no radius (!)) that has more than X people (will be configurable hard-coded in the explore mode meta-data).

    Now:
        Every location with music available will be visible in the following way:
            A constant circle waves every 1 seconds - with the color of blue - 100% same effect as the 
            green effect that already in the game (that showed when encounter a city with music)
            
        When user encounter the location - it will have 100% the same sound of encounter a city with music (target city),
        in a normal level (the tick sound and the music) and the green circles waves - that will be changed after that to blue
        circle waves - to show that this is an explore mode music location.

        In the HUD there will be a title:
            Last visited:                # <- instead of the fuel
               🏢 Paris, France

        This will be a list of 5 cities that are the latest that the payer has encountered with music.
        The list will be updated every time the player encounters a new city with music, showing the most recent one at the top.

        5 will be configurable in the meta-data as well.
