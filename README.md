# Echoes - navigate the world through sound
### [Link](https://dor-yeheskel.itch.io/echoes)
### [Gameplay Demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bRqHlcFKc1M)

## 🧭 Overview

✈️ A 2D flight experience focused on navigation  
🌍 Travel across real-world geography  
🎶 Music evolves with your journey  

ℹ️ For best visuals, make sure your browser zoom is set to 100%.  

## 🧭 Goal

Choose a route and navigate the world.

## 🌍 Explore Mode

Freely roam the world - no fuel, no restarts.  
Discover locations and hear their unique music as you arrive.

## ⚡ Game Feel

Calm. Focused. Continuous.
Navigation over action. No rush.

## 🎮 Controls

⬅️ ➡️ — Turn
